# Worked example · A session, start to finish

> how the skill works. The frameworks and quotes are real and cited (see `SOURCES.md`).

## What the founder said

> "I'm the CEO of Ledgerly, a 30-person B2B finance tool. My head of product, Priya, is great but always
> underwater. The team misses dates, every quarter planning turns into a fight, and my co-founder thinks we
> just need a better PM process. We also launch a big integrations push in 8 weeks. I spend my week in
> meetings."

## The session summary (the skill's output)

# Session: Ledgerly (CEO and head of product)

**The real problem:** you don't have a strategy the team uses to decide. The missed dates and planning fights
are symptoms. "Most execution problems... are either strategy problems or interpersonal problems or cultural
problems." [L1 00:54:49] Quick check: in the last three big prioritization calls, did anyone reference the
strategy? If not, "you don't have a strategy." [X 2021-08-09]

**Level mismatch?** Likely. You're arguing at the impact level ("is this the right bet?") while Priya
defends execution ("we can't hit that date"). "We litigate the minutiae... because we are default thinking at
different levels." [L1 00:48:46]

## Do this week

1. **Write the strategy one-pager together** (`templates/04-strategy-one-pager.md`): mission, vision,
   strategy, segmentation, positioning, roadmap, one line each [X 2021-11-17], plus customer motivation,
   differentiation and distribution [L2 19:32]. A process fix before this is a band-aid that will fall off.
   [L1 01:01:18]
2. **Run a pre-mortem on the integrations launch** (`templates/02-pre-mortem-kit.md`), with a separate one for
   go-to-market. [L1 00:30:07] Eight weeks out is the right time: "very low downside, but very high upside."
   [L1 00:33:00]
3. **Re-plan next quarter by opportunity cost**, not ROI (`templates/05-opportunity-cost-review.md`). Aim
   near his 60/30/10 split and allow "one or two" big initiatives, not five. [L1 01:09:45 to 01:10:43]

## The L task you're avoiding

The strategy doc itself. "Deep down, they don't know if they can formulate a good strategy. So time becomes a
convenient excuse." [L1 00:42:38] Book two hours somewhere other than the office. [L1 00:44:26]

## For Priya: "always underwater"

Run the LNO week (`templates/01-lno-week.md`). "No time" is often really "clarity on strategy" or "support
from management", two of his 8 hidden causes [X 2022-02-14], which item 1 above starts to fix.

## Questions to sit with

- "Is this the very best use of my time & energy?" [SS sometimes-you-should-talk-to-fewer]
- Which of your meetings this week are O tasks you could drop or delegate?
