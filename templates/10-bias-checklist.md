# Template 10 · Team bias checklist

Run it at the start of a big project, and again before a big decision. Based on
`references/11-clear-thinking-and-biases.md`. He talks about biases "at the level of teams", and puts the onus
on the leader to name them up front [AMP 00:54:37]. The checklist format is our suggestion.

Project / decision: ______________________  Leader: __________

## 1. Say this at the kickoff (our wording of his approach)

*As a group, we're likely to fall for some of these. We'll flag them when we see them. Flagging isn't a
weakness.*

## 2. The two he insists on [AMP 00:54:37]

- [ ] **Confirmation bias.** What evidence would change our mind? Have we looked for it?
- [ ] **Fundamental attribution bias.** Are we blaming a person for what the situation caused?
