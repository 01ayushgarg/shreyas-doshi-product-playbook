# 08 · High agency

> "High agency is about finding a way to get what you want without waiting for conditions to be perfect or
> otherwise blaming the circumstances." [L1 01:11:55] (He credits the term to Eric Weinstein.)

> "High Agency People either push through in the face of adverse conditions or manage to reverse the adverse
> conditions to achieve goals." [X 2020-06-27](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1276956838840295425)

## Its components [L1 01:13:56]

- "Ownership mindset is one component of high agency."
- "A creative execution is another aspect of high agency and they did that with a high degree of resilience,
  which is a third aspect of high agency."

## Needs judgment

> "Indeed, High Agency without Keen Judgment = Trainwreck" [X 2026-05-13](https://x.com/shreyas/status/2054620576346689844)

## His updated view, and the cost

In 2020 he wrote that high agency "can also be developed later in life."
[X 2020-06-27](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1276956836856393728) In 2026 he added two caveats:

> "1) Not everyone can develop High Agency—and High Agency people will find it hard to accept this 2) It has
> major professional upsides, but also creates chronic anxiety in life situations you can't control"
> [X 2026-05-12](https://x.com/shreyas/status/2054227795988124132)

> "One downside of being a very high agency person is that you are wired to never blame other people or bad
> luck, which then leads you to internalize failures & nasty jolts as being on you even though you may have
> tried your very best. Longterm, this is extremely emotionally taxing."
> [X 2022-09-23](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1573322882822053889)

A habit that balances it: "View your triumphs in 3rd person (humility) · View your worries in 2nd person
(perspective) · View your problems in 1st person (agency)" [X 2021-10-01](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1444009936569061376)

**Checks to run:**
1. Where are you waiting for conditions to be perfect?
2. Is your agency paired with judgment, or is it just motion?
3. Which failure are you carrying that wasn't really yours?
