# 03 · Impact, execution, optics: the three levels of product work

> "There are three levels of product work, impact, execution, and optics." [L1 00:46:12]

| Level | The question it asks |
|---|---|
| **Impact** | Does this change the business and users' lives? |
| **Execution** | Are we doing the work well and on time? |
| **Optics** | Do the right people know about it? |

(The question column is our summary; the three levels are his.)

## Most disagreements are level mismatches

> "The CEO is thinking at the impact level, you're thinking at the execution level, there is that mismatch."
> [L1 00:47:58]

> "We litigate the minutiae of whatever issue we are discussing, but we never really recognize that it's
> because we are default thinking at different levels." [L1 00:48:46]

**The move:** before arguing details, ask which level each person is at.

## Optics is real work, up to a point

> "Optics is about creating awareness of the impact and the execution that you're doing or your team is
> doing." [L1 00:50:06]

> "You should be spending some time on internal optics because it creates energy, it creates awareness, it
> creates excitement, it creates opportunities for feedback." [L1 00:50:06]

The failure mode: "optics sometimes becomes the goal." [L1 00:50:57] Related: "separate the effort involved in
building something with the effort you want to put behind talking about said thing." [L1 00:22:00]

As you rise: "as you go higher up in the corporate ladder, no matter what kind of company it is, you are now
going to have to spend a lot of time on optics, at the optics level." [L2 40:33]

## The right level depends on the stage

> "Most early stage teams should actually optimize for execution." [L1 00:52:23]

> "It's about being sensitive to what's right in this situation." [L1 00:53:20]

Know your default: "I have a preferred level at which I like to operate" [L2 39:32], and "willpower is finite"
[L2 40:33], so plan the time you spend outside it.

## Execution is not activity

> "Highly visible activity is not great execution. Seemingly intense activity is not great execution.
> Shipping on the committed date is not great execution. Great execution is ultimately about outstanding
> results & singular impact." [X 2024-07-13](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1812167751521517851)

**Checks to run:**
1. In your last tense meeting, which level was each person on?
2. What share of your week goes to impact, execution and optics? Does it fit your stage?
3. Is anything being done mainly for optics?
