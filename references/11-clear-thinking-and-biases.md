# 11 · Clear thinking and the biases that cloud it

> "It is about the quality of your thinking." [SS sometimes-you-should-talk-to-fewer]

## Explain by analogy, don't decide by it

> "You should explain by an evocative analogy (to help people understand your decisions) but you should not
> reason by an evocative analogy (to arrive at your decisions)" [SS the-antithesis-principle]

> "A clever analogy is never a good reason for why you should do something." [SS 10-ideas-for-2026]

> "The seductive appeal of feeling smart trounces the rational goal of making the correct decision."
> [SS 10-ideas-for-2026]

| Bias | In his words |
|---|---|

| Alliteration | "we get very impressed with alliterations" [L2 34:58] |
| "I already know that" | "the greatest invisible barrier" [SS 10-ideas-for-2026] |
| Apple pie positions | "A statement that instantly elevates the person who is saying it and is simultaneously hard for anyone else to push back on, and so everyone avoids the personal risk and just nods "yes"" [X 2023-02-23](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1628567045800591361) |
