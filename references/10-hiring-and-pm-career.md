# 10 · Hiring PMs, and growing as one

## What a PM is

His one-post definition: the role is to "Define the product & orchestrate actions across the org to enable
its success", measured by user adoption and satisfaction plus business impact. He lists the key skills as
critical thinking, cognitive empathy and influential communication, and the key traits as openness, deep care
and high agency. [X 2022-01-03](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1477797445169860610)

## Hiring: coachable, learnable, intrinsic

He sorts PM attributes into three groups [X 2022-09-28](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1574919727926812672):

| Group | His list |
|---|---|
| Coachable | tactics, process, critical thinking, communication, domain knowledge |
| Learnable | cognitive empathy, high agency, deep focus, judgment, creativity |
| Intrinsic | drive, integrity, curiosity, deep care, ownership |

**Our reading:** since the intrinsic column is the one you can't coach, screen hardest for it and plan to
coach the rest. That inference is ours; the post itself gives the three lists.

From his high-agency thread: he prefers "Go Getters over Frustrated Geniuses" and never compromises on
integrity. [X 2020-06-27, 5/20](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1276956842602541056)

## The MSN list, before the job description

> "Before you create the JD, create your MSN list for the role." [X 2023-05-02](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1653199507042234368)

M is must have, S should have, N nice to have. His rules in the same post: at most three bullets each, every
bullet an atomic attribute, and every bullet something you can evaluate in the process (resume, interviews,
reference checks).

## The abilities that compound

He ranks a dozen "very high leverage abilities for PMs" from easier to harder to acquire, starting with
domain knowledge, deep focus and UX evaluation, and ending with high agency, curiosity and creativity.
[X 2023-03-28](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1640660059280781312)

## Permit yourself

The context matters for this line. The post begins "You don't need anyone's permission to build these
superpowers as a PM" and lists nine: ownership mindset, open-mindedness, nuanced thinking, self-regulation,
kindness, persuasion, presence, tenacity and high agency. Then: "Knowledge is mostly commoditized. So to
differentiate, permit yourself." [X 2021-11-22](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1462832084129513472) So
“permit yourself” means: build those traits without waiting for a title or a manager's say-so.

## Content, confidence, context

He describes three things you're judged on [X 2024-09-06](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1831859610715869523):
**Content** (your insights, decisions, results), **Confidence** (the image you project), **Context** (your
read of the culture and power structure). "To have a chance of getting Director / VP level scope, you must be
very good at a minimum of 2 of these and you must not suck at the 3rd one."

## Mindset, principles, tactics

Many people "get stuck at tactics for the majority, if not the entirety, of their career." Principles let you
choose and create tactics; mindset is the most important layer. [AMP 00:30:29] Wanting step-by-step recipes for
inherently ambiguous situations (strategy, prioritization, managing up) is, he says, "actually an implicit
test of your suitability for senior PM leadership roles."
[X 2024-02-02](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1753496900584837457)

## How to apply it to a hire (our reading)

1. **Write the MSN list** (`templates/06-msn-hiring-list.md`) before the job description.
2. **Tag each bullet** coachable, learnable or intrinsic.
3. **Move coachable items down** to S or N unless you truly can't coach them in time.
4. **Assign an evaluation** to each M: which interview, work sample or reference question tests it.
5. **Debrief by bullet,** not by overall impression.

**Worked scenario (invented):** a founder's first draft of M is fintech experience, SQL and roadmap tools. All
three are coachable. The revised M: ownership (reference check on a project that went wrong), curiosity
(interview on a domain they learned fast), and integrity (reference check). Fintech experience moves to N.

**Failure modes (our reading):** a Must list of tools and domains; bullets that bundle two attributes; no
plan for how to test a bullet; hiring a “Frustrated Genius” because the resume is strong.
