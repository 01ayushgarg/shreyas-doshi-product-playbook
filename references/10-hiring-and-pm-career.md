# 10 · Hiring PMs, and growing as one

## What a PM is

> "Product Management. Role: Define the product & orchestrate actions across the org to enable its success.
> Success: User adoption & satisfaction · Business impact. Key skills: Critical thinking · Cognitive empathy ·
> Influential communication. Key traits: Openness · Deep care · High agency"
> [X 2022-01-03](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1477797445169860610)

## Hiring: coachable, learnable, intrinsic

> "Coachable: Tactics · Process · Critical thinking · Communication · Domain knowledge. Learnable: Cognitive
> empathy · High agency · Deep focus · Judgment · Creativity. Intrinsic: Drive · Integrity · Curiosity · Deep
> care · Ownership" [X 2022-09-28](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1574919727926812672)

**The move:** screen hard for the intrinsic column; coach the rest.

## The MSN list, before the job description

> "Before you create the JD, create your MSN list for the role. M = Must have · S = Should have · N = Nice to
> have... 1) Max 3 bullets each for M/S/N 2) Each bullet must be an atomic attribute 3) You can evaluate each
> bullet in the hiring process (resume, interviews, reference checks)"
> [X 2023-05-02](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1653199507042234368)

## The abilities that compound

> "Very high leverage abilities for PMs: (roughly sorted by easier → more difficult to acquire) Domain
> knowledge · Deep focus · UX evaluation · Product prioritization · Product strategy · Cognitive empathy ·
> Clear thinking · Simulation · Influential communication · High agency · Curiosity · Creativity"
> [X 2023-03-28](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1640660059280781312)

> "Knowledge is mostly commoditized. So to differentiate, permit yourself."
> [X 2021-11-22](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1462832084129513472)

## Content, confidence, context

He describes three things you're judged on [X 2024-09-06](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1831859610715869523):
**Content** (your insights, decisions, results), **Confidence** (the image you project), **Context** (your
read of the culture and power structure). "To have a chance of getting Director / VP level scope, you must be
very good at a minimum of 2 of these and you must not suck at the 3rd one."

## Competence first

> "Thought leadership without sufficient competence may fool some, but it does not fool those with actual
> competence... So focus first on competence." [X 2022-09-13](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1569833006269100033)

## Mid-career

> "Truth is that the ceiling is far higher than you think." [SS the-ceiling-is-far-higher-than-you]

> "Mid-career onwards, ambitious people usually ask "what should I do?" but they almost never ask "what should
> I learn?"" [SS the-ceiling-is-far-higher-than-you]

> "Because when it comes to your career, you are the user." [SS on-mid-career-satisfaction]

> "Identify your superpowers, because if you identify your superpowers and work in accordance with them, you
> will do the best work of your life." [L2 42:34]

Senior roles test comfort with ambiguity: wanting "detailed step by step recipes & tactics for inherently
ambiguous situations e.g., strategy, prioritization, managing up... is actually an implicit test of your
suitability for senior PM leadership roles." [X 2024-02-02](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1753496900584837457)
