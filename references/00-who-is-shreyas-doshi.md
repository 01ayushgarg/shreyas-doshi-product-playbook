# 00 · Who Shreyas Doshi is

A product leader best known for turning hard-won lessons from Stripe, Twitter, Google and Yahoo into
frameworks product people actually use: LNO, pre-mortems, the three levels of product work, and "most
execution problems are strategy problems".

- **Career, from his own descriptions:** product roles at Yahoo, Google and Twitter, then Stripe, where he
  led product for Stripe Connect and Stripe Terminal and was Stripe's first PM manager (his Maven course
  page). He now writes, teaches and advises.
- **Where his ideas live:** two Lenny's Podcast episodes (2022, and live in 2024), a free Substack (since
  December 2025), and years of threads on X (@shreyas).

## Why his advice is worth reading

- It is built for the situations product people actually face: too many meetings, unclear strategy, an exec
  who says everything is a priority, a launch that might fail.
- It is honest about its own limits. He updates his views in public, for example on whether everyone can
  develop high agency ([08-high-agency.md](08-high-agency.md)).

## Read him with his own caveat

> "Frameworks are intuition, packaged... make the framework work for you, and make sure you don't work for
> the framework." [X 2023-07-19](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1681697116027068418)
