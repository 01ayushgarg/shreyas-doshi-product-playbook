## The meetings he ran at Stripe

From his list of "10 high leverage meetings I swore by... as I learned how to lead product for a couple of
Stripe's fast-growing products" [X 2023-05-03](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1653611053442555904):

1. EOD sync with Eng counterpart
2. Weekly Sales Sync
3. 2:2s with EM & PM of each team
4. Monthly Execution Review
5. Biweekly Business Growth Meeting
6. Monthly career chat with my directs
7. Product deep-dives
8. Pre-mortems for big launches
9. New Team Member Intro / Boba Party
10. Birthday Celebration

## Stay humble, stay learnable

> "Imagine yourself as a cup that is ¾ full." [SS dont-be-a-full-cup]

> "A more reliable sign of humility is an unperformative willingness to learn from someone whom we don't deem
> 'superior' to us." [SS the-humility-trap]
