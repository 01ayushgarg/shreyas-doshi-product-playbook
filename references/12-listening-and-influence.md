# 12 · Listening, influence and stakeholders

> a few years ago. Night & day difference in my leadership ability."
> [X 2021-01-17](https://x.com/shreyas/status/1350734022868045825)

> "Partition listening and thinking." [SS becoming-great-at-listening]

> "Your job as a listener is to consume in a few seconds or minutes what the speaker might have experienced
> over weeks, months, even decades." [SS becoming-great-at-listening]
